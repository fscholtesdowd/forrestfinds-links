Privacy Policy

Forrest Finds Poster is a small tool run by Forrest Scholtes-Dowd. It is used only by him, to post his own videos to his own TikTok accounts.

What it keeps: the login token TikTok gives after the owner says yes, and the basic profile info TikTok shares (account name and picture). It keeps nothing about other people.

How it is used: only to upload and post the owner's videos and to show which account is connected.

Sharing: nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Removal: the owner can disconnect the tool in TikTok's settings at any time; the stored token is then deleted.

Contact: open an issue at github.com/fscholtesdowd/forrestfinds-links.
